TURQUALITY® Consulting

Human resources and organization consulting for companies preparing for the program

TURQUALITY® is a state-supported branding program run by the Ministry of Trade of the Republic of Türkiye and aiming to help Turkish brands gain sustainable competitiveness in international markets. The program expects maturity in areas such as institutionalization, management systems, human resources and brand management.

This page describes the consulting offered to companies preparing for the TURQUALITY® process on the human resources and organization side.

Why human resources?

What does HR show in TURQUALITY® assessments?

In assessments, human resources is treated not merely as an operational function but as a critical management system that supports company strategy. Areas such as talent management, the performance system, leadership development and organizational structure carry significant weight.

What audits really look at is not only whether processes are defined and procedures complete, but how deeply these processes have been internalized. We shared the HR and organization readiness checklist and the 2026 support limits in the article Human Resources Consulting for TURQUALITY®.
