Assessment Center Coaching

Preparation for Assessment Center processes and corporate consulting

An Assessment Center is an evaluation process that measures not so much a manager's current performance as whether they are ready for higher and more complex leadership roles. Its results are used in decisions on promotion, development planning and whether a person is ready for the next position.

This page covers two types of support: one-to-one coaching for aspiring managers who will go through the process, and consulting for companies that want to set up or develop the process in their own organization.

Process

What is evaluated in an Assessment Center?

Assessment Centers use simulations close to real work situations: role plays, strategic planning exercises, competency-based interviews (often using the STAR technique), general aptitude tests and personality inventories. Observers usually assess the candidate against the company's own leadership competencies: effective communication, decision-making, taking initiative, and coaching and mentoring skills.

The article What Is an Assessment & Development Center? explains which seven indicators are used to read high potential and why performance and potential are not the same thing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Assessment Center Coaching

An Assessment Center is a practice that measures not so much a person's current performance as their potential to take on higher and more complex leadership roles in the future. Assessment is carried out through simulations close to real work situations, using tools such as role plays, strategic planning exercises, competency-based interviews, aptitude tests and personality inventories.
It is suitable for professionals and aspiring managers who will go through an Assessment Center process for a promotion or a managerial position. It is designed for those who want to clarify their strengths and development areas before the process, understand the company's competency model and prepare for the simulations through practice.
The process is evaluated not through knowledge alone but through case analysis, role play, group exercises and competency-based observation. In preparation, it is important to understand the company's competency model, become familiar with the inventory tools and practise the simulations. In one-to-one coaching sessions, the candidate's strengths and development areas are identified and a personalized preparation program is created.
Consulting is offered on Assessment Center design, competency model development and assessor training. Thanks to more than 25 years of HR experience, a holistic view is provided for both the candidate side and the organization side.
Both formats are available. Face-to-face meetings can be held in Istanbul; online work is available across Türkiye and abroad. Corporate programs can also be held at the company's office.
The first meeting is free of charge. In this meeting, your needs are heard and the most suitable program is determined together.
