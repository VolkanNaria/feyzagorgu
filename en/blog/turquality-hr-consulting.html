If your company wants to build a brand and grow abroad, did you know that a significant portion of certain expenses it makes is supported by the state? As of 2026, support limits have risen to quite high levels.

1. Key Financial Advantages

Support AreaAdvantage Provided
Advertising & promotion abroadGenerally, 50% of expenses can be supported
Trademark / patent / design registrationSupport for registration and protection costs abroad
Rent for offices/stores abroadSupport within specified conditions and limits
Store/office setup & decoration abroadCertain expenses may be covered by the support
ConsultingCorporate infrastructure and target market consulting can be supported
EmploymentCertain qualified personnel expenses under the structuring abroad can be supported
Market research / reportsResearch and reporting expenses for target markets
Trade fairsTrade fairs and promotional activities abroad
Development roadmapThe initial-stage development roadmap study can be supported

In the current regulations of the Ministry of Trade of the Republic of Türkiye, the support items and application requirements for TURQUALITY® and the Brand Program are defined separately.

For detailed and up-to-date information, see the Ministry of Trade website.

2. What Are the Figures in 2026?

2026 TURQUALITY® annual total support ceiling: TRY 493,271,347
  • Development Roadmap: TRY 11,836,111
  • Consulting expenses / employment in design and product development: TRY 49,325,771
  • TURQUALITY® annual total support: TRY 493,271,347

These limits are included in the support ceilings published by the Ministry of Trade for 2026. However, TRY 493 million is not money paid directly into the company's account. The support mechanism operates on the expenses the company incurs that are deemed eligible under the legislation.

For detailed and up-to-date information, see the Ministry of Trade website.

3. Where Is the Biggest Financial Benefit for the Company?

The real financial value of TURQUALITY lies in "the state sharing the growth investment of an exporting company." For example, suppose a company spends TRY 10 million on international advertising, TRY 5 million on consulting, TRY 8 million on trademarks/patents abroad, TRY 12 million on offices/stores abroad, and TRY 5 million on trade fairs and marketing to expand abroad. When these fall within eligible expenses and the applicable support rates/limits, a significant portion of this can be supported, and the company's net cost of growing abroad can fall considerably.

4. Indirect Financial Advantage

TURQUALITY should not be thought of merely as "state support." The program aims to make the company more institutional across the dimensions of strategy → brand → organization → finance → sales → marketing → human resources → digital infrastructure → operations abroad. For this reason, especially in medium and large-scale manufacturing/exporting companies, operational efficiency stands out alongside financial support.

Human Resources Consulting for TURQUALITY®

In companies that are usually founded as start-ups and later move into the growth stage, we observe some very fundamental problems: the lack of an institutional structure, unclear boundaries of authority and responsibility, a performance management system that is not used effectively, and talent management that does not function properly.

The questions company owners ask most often are these: "We have managed our company this far without any of these, so why are they needed now? Our most important priority is increasing revenue and profitability, so why should we prioritize these?"

In fact, TURQUALITY is at the same point. They want to support companies' investments, especially abroad, for profitable growth, but they have one condition: it must be sustainable. And the most important condition of sustainability is a high level of trust in the company culture.

In a company where duties and responsibilities as well as authorities are defined, where there is a fair performance management system internally, and where those who deserve it are promoted, employees are motivated to do their best and their commitment increases, which reflects positively on the company's profitability and customer satisfaction. As the company grows, the founders' natural span of control narrows — so it is necessary to build systems to manage these processes and to run them within a culture of trust.

This is what TURQUALITY audits really look at: as much as having defined processes and complete procedures, how deeply these processes have been internalized also draws the auditors' attention. In short, the companies that pass these audits are those that do not just get through the day but set a long-term vision, build sustainable policies, and keep a culture of trust alive for that purpose — in other words, companies with a strong "immune system."

For years, as a Human Resources professional, I thought of a culture of trust as something there was either more or less of, but now I understand that the opposite of trust is not distrust, but fear. If you think there is a certain level of trust within your company but do not know where to start, structuring your Human Resources processes is a subject we would very much enjoy working on.

TURQUALITY® HR and Organizational Readiness Checklist

1. Strategy and Organization

  • The company has a written strategic plan
  • The organization chart is up to date
  • Job descriptions are written and up to date
  • An authority and responsibility matrix (RACI, etc.) is defined
  • Department KPIs are linked to company goals
  • Regular management meetings are held
  • Process owners have been defined

2. Human Resources Infrastructure

  • HR policies and procedures are written
  • The recruitment process is standardized
  • A competency model has been created
  • Position-based competency sets exist
  • A compensation system is defined
  • An HR information system (HRIS) is used
  • Workforce planning is carried out

3. Performance Management

  • A company KPI system exists
  • Individual goals are set
  • Annual performance evaluation is carried out
  • Performance results feed into development plans
  • Performance results are linked to the reward system
  • Managers' performance is evaluated separately

4. Training and Development

  • An annual training plan exists
  • Training needs analysis is carried out
  • Leadership development programs exist
  • Manager development programs are implemented
  • Training effectiveness is measured
  • An internal trainer system exists
  • Competency-based development plans are prepared

5. Talent Management and Succession

  • Critical positions have been identified
  • High-potential employees have been identified
  • Backup plans have been prepared
  • Career paths have been defined
  • A talent assessment system exists
  • Manager development programs are implemented

Correctly identifying high-potential employees is one of the most critical steps of TURQUALITY preparation. In this respect, Assessment & Development Center practices offer an objective and structured method.

6. Culture and Leadership

  • Company values have been defined
  • Values have been translated into behavioral indicators
  • A leadership model has been defined
  • An employee engagement survey is conducted
  • Action plans are followed up
  • Internal communication processes are run regularly

7. Digitalization and People Analytics

  • An ERP system is used
  • An HRIS exists
  • HR dashboards are in place
  • Human capital KPIs are tracked
  • Workforce turnover rate is analyzed
  • Training, performance and talent data are reported
  • AI-powered HR applications are used

Documents to Have Ready for the Audit

  • Organization: Organization chart, job descriptions, authority matrix
  • Performance: KPI structure, performance evaluation forms, goal cards
  • Training: Training matrix, annual training plan, attendance and effectiveness reports
  • Talent Management: Critical position list, succession plans, career maps
  • Culture and Leadership: Values booklet, leadership model, employee engagement results
  • People Analytics: Turnover reports, headcount reports, human capital KPI dashboards
