Culture and Leadership Transformation

Artificial intelligence is already driving major changes in organization design. According to a global survey conducted in 2026, nearly all senior executives (99%) expect headcount to fall by up to 20% over the next two years because AI is replacing jobs. In light of this, our reimagined services are:

◈

People and Culture Transformation Program

Tailoring organizational changes to the company for the shift from functional organizations to agile teams and from the traditional approach to agile structures. Analysis and headcount norm optimization for the shift from traditional roles to agile roles alongside AI.

◈

Performance Management System Consulting

Defining company-wide KPIs for the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) system that is widespread in agile cultures. Building an OKR, balanced scorecard, competency-based and goal-based performance management system according to the company culture.

◈

Building Incentive (Bonus) Systems

Designing and building short- and long-term incentive systems, specific to the market and the company, based on the results of the performance management system and in support of agile management.

◈

Talent Management

Designing the tools needed to measure leadership potential, and reviewing and redesigning the talent management system in line with the agile company culture and developments in artificial intelligence.

◈

Assessment Center Coaching

Analysis of company competencies, personality inventory, preparation for Assessment Center exercises, analytical tools and coaching sessions. One-to-one support in preparing aspiring managers for evaluation processes.

About

Corporate Experience,
Coaching Expertise

After graduating on a full scholarship from the Faculty of Business Administration at Bilkent University, I began my career at the Akbank Board of Inspectors. After five years in banking, I moved into Human Resources and Training.

I have worked for more than 25 years in both multinational companies and leading Turkish firms. In the final stage of my career, I served as HR Director and Executive Committee member at Liberty Sigorta and played an active role in M&A processes.

In 2021, I founded Narina Eğitim ve Danışmanlık and began serving individuals and organizations as a coach, trainer and consultant in leadership development.
