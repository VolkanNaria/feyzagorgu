TURQUALITY® — 13 Questions

TURQUALITY® is the world's first state-supported branding program, run by the Ministry of Trade of the Republic of Türkiye, that aims to help Turkish brands gain sustainable competitiveness in international markets. The program aims to develop companies' capabilities in institutionalization, management systems, human resources, brand management and international growth.

Companies with the following characteristics are generally suitable candidates for the program:

  • Companies that export,
  • that own their own brand,
  • that invest in institutionalization,
  • that aim for sustainable growth,
  • and that want to develop their human resources and management systems.

Eligibility can be determined through a preliminary assessment.

Yes.
