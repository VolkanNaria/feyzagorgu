Training

Finance and Data-Driven Decision Making for Non-Finance Managers

A 2–3 day corporate training program for managers

You do not need to be a finance specialist to defend a number in a budget meeting, spot an illusion in a table, or choose between two alternatives, but you do need to read figures correctly and question them. This training gives managers without a finance background exactly that.

The training is delivered on behalf of Narina Eğitim ve Danışmanlık by Feyza Görgü, who has more than 25 years of experience in Finance and Human Resources.

What you learn

Four foundations of thinking in numbers

The training combines analytical thinking, basic statistics, basic finance and accounting, and a case study that brings them together.

  • Questioning illusions in data: mean, median and reading tables
  • Telling correlation apart from cause and effect
  • Time value of money, net present value and opportunity cost
  • Reading a balance sheet, income statement and cash flow statement
  • Budgeting, unit cost and profitability calculations
  • Building alternatives, making a decision and presenting it

Program

A sample 2–3 day flow

Duration and scope are set according to the company's needs and the participant group. Sample flow:

Day 1 · Learning to think and read data

  • Analytical thinking competency
  • Basic math and statistics: arithmetic mean, median, reading tables
  • Data and illusions, correlation and cause and effect

Day 2 · Basic finance and accounting

  • Time value of money, net present value, opportunity cost
  • Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement
  • Budget and profitability: revenue, cost, profit

Day 3 · SWOT simulation and case analysis

  • SWOT analysis exercise
  • Case: calculations, alternatives, decision making
  • Presenting the decision

Who it is for

People who have to decide with numbers

Participants

  • Department managers and team leaders
  • Specialists from HR, sales, operations and marketing
  • People without a finance background who carry budget and profitability responsibility

Method

  • Hands-on: calculation questions built on retail and sales examples
  • Group work
  • A case that ends in a decision and a presentation
